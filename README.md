# Magellan Health (magellan-health)

Magellan Health is a healthcare company focused on managing the fastest-growing, most complex areas of health, including special populations, complete pharmacy benefits, and other specialty areas of healthcare. Magellan Health was acquired by Centene Corporation in January 2022 and operates as a Centene subsidiary.
